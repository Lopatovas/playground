## The expansion mindset

Stages 0–4 built the stack — UI, API, database. Stage 5 deployed it; Stage 6 added tests. Stage 7 is where it becomes a **product**:

- Users sign in
- Data belongs to someone
- Lists grow beyond one screen
- Features ship on branches, not reboots

**Same repository. Same Git history. New capabilities.**

This is what professional engineering looks like — evolution, not tutorial reset.

## What you'll add

| Feature | Why it matters |

| **User-scoped data** | My workouts ≠ your workouts |
| **Related entities** | Categories, tags, comments — foreign keys |

You won't build everything on the internet. You'll build **one coherent expansion** that proves you can grow a system.
